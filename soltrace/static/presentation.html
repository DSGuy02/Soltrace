The Solution · 3 Simple Steps

How SolTrace Works in 3 Everyday Steps

An invisible assistant running in the background. No crypto wallet or blockchain knowledge needed.

STEP 1

Seal the Original

When an invoice is approved, stock is received, or an order is placed, SolTrace creates a permanent digital fingerprint and saves it to Solana in 0.4 seconds.

✔ Costs ~$0.00025 (less than 1/40th of a cent). Never slows down checkout.
STEP 2

Someone Edits the Database

An insider or hacker secretly changes the recipient bank account, increases an invoice total, or lowers a stock count inside the company database.

⚠ The regular app thinks all is fine, but the fingerprint no longer matches.
STEP 3

Check Against Solana

SolTrace compares the live database record with the fingerprint saved on Solana. If even one character changed, it immediately shows the difference in red.

✔ Blocks the fraudulent payment and gives the business 100% proof.
